# Choro Songbook

Chord charts for the 295 pieces of the three volumes of the *Choro Songbook*
(Almir Chediak, Mário Sève, Rogério Souza and Dininho; Lumiar Editora, 2009 and
2011), as transcribed in the DCML Choro Songbooks Corpus
(https://github.com/DCMLab/choro), by Fabian C. Moss and Willian Fernandes
Souza at the Digital and Cognitive Musicology Lab, EPFL.

The corpus is licensed CC BY-NC-SA 4.0
(https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/4.0/), and so is this book: it
may be shared and adapted for non-commercial use, with attribution, under the
same licence.

Each song has its part sections (A, B, C, ...), a Form line with the order they
are played in, and the corpus's local keys as Key notes. The original
transcriptions write a diminished chord as `o`; here it is `°`.
